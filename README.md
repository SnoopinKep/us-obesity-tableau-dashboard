# U.S. Adult Obesity Dashboard (Tableau)

Interactive Tableau dashboard of U.S. adult obesity by state using CDC BRFSS data.

**Live dashboard:** [View on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/shared/7GQDZ67XX)

- **Question:** How has adult obesity prevalence changed across U.S. states, and where is it highest and lowest?
- **Data:** CDC BRFSS, Nutrition, Physical Activity, and Obesity (data.cdc.gov)
- **Tools:** Tableau Public

## What it shows
- State map of obesity prevalence with a year selector
- Ranked states (top 10)
- Trend over time for selected states
- 4 KPIs: average rate, highest state, lowest state, change since 2011

## Key findings
- The average state obesity rate rose about 6.1 percentage points from 2011 to 2025.
- In 2024, West Virginia had the highest rate (41.4%) and Colorado the lowest (25.0%), a gap of about 16 points.

## Notes
- Values are self-reported height and weight.
- The average is the mean of state values, not population-weighted.
- States without data for a selected year are excluded.
